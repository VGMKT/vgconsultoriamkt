---
name: Consent for lead geolocation
description: Defines the consent boundary for geographic lead attribution.
---

Location is optional lead-attribution data and must only be captured after an explicit browser permission request initiated by the visitor.

**Why:** The business chose useful regional context without silently collecting a visitor's coordinates.

**How to apply:** Keep location sharing separate from form submission, make clear that the form works without it, and keep production document permissions compatible with browser geolocation.
